# NEXUS-X — 3-Minute SIH Demonstration Sequence

Total runtime target: **2:30–3:00**. Practice this once before judging — every
step below hits a real, working feature; nothing is scripted/fake.

## 0:00 – 0:15 — Login
- Open `http://localhost:5173`.
- Point out the demo-auth banner ("PROTOTYPE / DEMO AUTHENTICATION").
- Sign in with `investigator@nexusx.demo` / `demo123`.

## 0:15 – 0:35 — Overview
- Land on **Overview**. Call out the live counts: entities analyzed,
  relationships, AI hypotheses, contradictions.
- Say: *"Nothing here is a mockup number — every card is computed from the
  graph on page load."*
- Click **OPEN INTELLIGENCE GRAPH**.

## 0:35 – 1:00 — Intelligence Graph
- Show the heterogeneous graph (people, phones, accounts, locations, events).
- Point at the legend: solid green = Confirmed, dashed amber = AI Hypothesis.
  *"AI hypotheses are never drawn to look like confirmed evidence."*
- Click a node (e.g. Priya Chatterjee) → side panel shows network role,
  betweenness centrality, connected entities.

## 1:00 – 1:20 — Story Mode (fastest way to show breadth)
- Switch to **Story Mode** → **START CASE RECONSTRUCTION**.
- Let it step through: FIR received → entities extracted → CDR added →
  financial data added → graph expands → hidden link discovered →
  contradiction detected → temporal shift → counterfactual bridge →
  final hypothesis. All ten steps pull from the live computed graph.

## 1:20 – 1:45 — Hidden-Link Hypothesis Engine
- Switch to **Hypotheses**.
- Open **VIEW SCORE BREAKDOWN** on the top hypothesis — show the six
  weighted, explainable features (common neighbors, temporal overlap,
  shared locations, communication correlation, financial correlation,
  source reliability).
- Click **Show Graph Path** to trace the connecting chain.

## 1:45 – 2:05 — Contradiction Detection & Counterfactual Simulation
- Switch to **Analytics**.
- Show the flagged **Intelligence Conflict** (same person, two locations,
  minutes apart) with its possible explanations.
- Run the **Counterfactual Simulator**: pick the highest-betweenness entity,
  click **REMOVE FROM ANALYTICAL GRAPH**, show Before → After components,
  density, and the new bridge candidate. Emphasize: *"No data is deleted —
  this is a simulation."*

## 2:05 – 2:25 — AI Copilot
- Switch to **AI Copilot**.
- Click the suggested chip *"Why is Priya Chatterjee structurally important?"*
- Then type a custom question, e.g. *"Which hypotheses have contradictory
  evidence?"* — show the answer is grounded with confidence basis and
  evidence references, not a generic LLM reply.

## 2:25 – 2:45 — Report Generation
- Switch to **Reports** → **GENERATE INVESTIGATION INTELLIGENCE REPORT**.
- Scroll to the disclaimer at the bottom: *"AI-generated hypotheses are
  investigative leads and must not be treated as established facts without
  independent verification."*

## 2:45 – 3:00 — Close
- Return to **Overview**.
- Closing line: *"NEXUS-X doesn't just show investigators what's known — it
  shows what might be missing, why, and how confident the system is, while
  keeping a human in the loop at every step."*
